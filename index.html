Who We Are

Four things that shape every visit, from the name on the door to the ingredients on the plate.

01

A Playful Nod to Pie and Precision

The bar’s name, 3.14 Pi, is a clever tribute to both the mathematical constant and our love for pies. This symbolism reflects our passion for precision and creativity, evident in every dish we serve. It’s a name that represents not just our food, but our dedication to making every experience memorable.

02

Experience the Magic of 3.14 Pi Bar

From our handcrafted pies to our commitment to community, every detail at 3.14 Pi Bar is designed with you in mind. We’re passionate about what we do, and we’re excited to share it with you. Come by and experience the comfort, creativity, and connection that make 3.14 Pi Bar truly special.

03

A Space for Making Memories

At 3.14 Pi Bar, we don’t just serve food. We create experiences. Whether you’re celebrating a special occasion, catching up with old friends, or enjoying a quiet moment to yourself, we’re here to make your visit special. Our cozy ambiance and attentive team ensure that every guest feels at home.

04

Supporting Local and Sustainable Practices

We’re proud to support local businesses by sourcing ingredients from nearby suppliers whenever possible. Sustainability is also important to us, and we’re always looking for ways to minimize waste and reduce our environmental footprint. It’s just one way we show our love for the Colorado Springs community.

Our Atmosphere

A Space for Making Memories

Cozy tables, warm lighting, and a full bar under one roof, indoors and on the patio. Come celebrate, catch up with friends, or grab a quiet booth of your own.
